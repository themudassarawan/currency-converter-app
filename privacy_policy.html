Privacy Policy

Currency Converter by MMA

Effective Date: October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Currency Converter by MMA mobile application ("App") handles information.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We respect your privacy. The App does not collect, track, transmit, or store any personal data, user identifiers, device location, contacts, or financial transaction logs.

2. Network and API Communications

To provide real-time currency conversions, the App connects to public exchange rate endpoints via HTTPS. These requests contain only the currency pairs requested and do not transmit any identifiable personal data.

3. Local Storage

Any local preferences, custom rates, or conversion history entries are stored locally on your device storage only and are never uploaded to any external server.

4. Children's Privacy

Our App is suitable for general audiences of all ages and does not knowingly collect information from children.
